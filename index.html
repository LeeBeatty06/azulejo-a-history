Art of the Azulejos

What are Portuguese Azulejos?

Across Portugal, azulejos have transformed the country's architecture into surfaces filled with patterns, colors, imagery, and history. This art form grew over years and years of creative sharing. Portuguese tilework started from repeating geometric patterns, pictorial panels, architectural facades, individual tiles, and now into modern-day designs.

Craftsmanship and Origins

Process

The traditional glazed ceramic tiles are made using multiple steps. First, they are shaped with red clay, molded into squares, and dried for weeks. They are then fired, glazed with a tin-oxide base and an opaque white layer, hand-painted with raw metal oxide pigments, and fired once more for their final finish.
